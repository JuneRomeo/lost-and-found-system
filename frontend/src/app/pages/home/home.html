Lost Something? Found Something?

Welcome to Araneta Lost and Found. Start by browsing Found Items in the top navigation, or select an option below to report an item or track an existing claim.

Report a Lost Item

Have you lost something? Submit a report to help others identify your missing belongings and contact you if they are found.

How to report:

  1. Open the Lost Item reporting form.
  2. Enter the item's name, description, and category.
  3. Provide the date and location where you lost it.
  4. Upload a photo if available.
  5. Submit your report.

Report a Found Item

Found something that might belong to someone else? Submit a report so its owner can identify and recover it.

How to report:

  1. Open the Found Item reporting form.
  2. Enter the item's name, description, and category.
  3. Provide where and when you found it.
  4. Upload a photo if available.
  5. Submit your report and follow the turnover instructions.

Track My Claim

Already submitted a claim for a lost item? Use your claim reference number to check the current status of your claim and see whether it has been approved or rejected.

How to track your claim:

  1. Open the Claim Tracker.
  2. Enter your claim reference number.
  3. Submit the reference number.
  4. View your claim status.
